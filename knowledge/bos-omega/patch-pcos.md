# BOS-OMEGA Patch — PCOS / ANS

Authority rank: **Patch**. Outranked only by Canon. Outranks Continuity, Logs, Conversation, and Inference.

PCOS here is the BOS-OMEGA Patch for autonomic state, not a medical diagnosis engine. Use it to classify load and choose Recovery vs Performance. It does not prescribe drugs or treatment.

## ANS states

The autonomic nervous system (ANS) in this Patch has five named states:

1. **Sleep** — offline restoration. No performance demand. Growth processes that require stillness happen here. Forcing Praxis during Sleep is a Patch violation.
2. **Parasympathetic** — rest-and-digest. Low threat, high recovery. Good for ingest, review, and Continuity writing. Poor for sprint execution.
3. **Functional Sympathetic** — mobilized and precise. The lawful performance band: stress is present, recovery is scheduled, output is high without freeze or panic.
4. **Hyper Sympathetic** — over-mobilized. Tunnel vision, repeated identical retries, skipped Omega verification. Control-loop analogue: LOOP_DETECTED / UNSTABLE. Required move is Recovery, not more explanation.
5. **Freeze** — immobilized under load. No Praxis, no Omega. Control-loop analogue: BLOCKED. Required move is safety and a smaller next GO, not a larger plan.

## Recovery ↔ Performance oscillation

Performance is not a steady state. The Patch requires oscillation:

- **Performance** lives in Functional Sympathetic: execute, retrieve, verify, ship.
- **Recovery** lives in Parasympathetic and Sleep: ingest, rest, write Continuity, lower free energy.

Staying in Performance without Recovery produces Hyper Sympathetic then Freeze. Staying in Recovery without Performance produces no Omega outcomes. The lawful waveform is Recovery ↔ Performance, not a monotone.

## Stress + recovery = growth

Growth is the product of a stressor **and** a completed recovery cycle. Stress without recovery is damage (Hyper Sympathetic / Freeze). Recovery without a prior stressor is idle. This is the Patch equation:

`growth = stress × recovery` (both terms required; either term alone is ~0)

Operator application: a hard retrieve/fix/test is the stressor; a passing test plus a written Continuity note is the recovery. Do not claim growth from conversation alone.
